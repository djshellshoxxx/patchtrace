## Original reproduction, before the repair

With two matching records `exampledrv.sys@2099-02-12.json` and
`exampledrv.sys@2099-02-12.json.gz` containing distinct synthetic
payloads, the pre-change method returned a selected path instead of refusing
the ambiguous source. With a matching record symbolic link to bytes outside
the workspace index directory, it followed the alias and returned the link
as a selected candidate.

An isolated verbatim reproduction against the old selection method printed:

```text
same-date different bytes: selected= exampledrv.sys@2099-02-12.json.gz refused= False
symlink to outside: selected= exampledrv.sys@2099-02-12.json refused= False
```

These are controlled local synthetic source inputs, not retrieved vendor
metadata, nor target binaries. `max(..., key=date)` selects the first record
encountered on a same-date tie, making the chosen content dependent on the
directory enumeration. The fix selects only a unique most-recent date, rejects
a matching source alias before reading its data, and preserves the existing
missing-record behavior. A sole newest date still wins over older conflicting
records. The method does not make replacement after selection impossible.
