## Packet 1: isotonic plateaus, unequal weights and selective coverage

Owner seam: `tools/calibration/mathcore.fit_isotonic`, `metrics.{auc,ece_equal_mass,coverage_precision_curve}`, `patchtrace.calibration.predict.apply_isotonic`. Extend mathematical tests rather than create training artifacts. Budget: at most eight rows and all unique permutations of four rows.

1. Scores `[0.1,0.2,0.3]`, outcomes `[0,1,0]`, unit weights, `clip=False`: fitted knots are `[(0.1,0),(0.2,0.5),(0.3,0.5)]`; runtime predictions are `[0,0.5,0.5]`. AUC changes from `0.5` to `0.75`. Assert monotonic predictions, train/runtime interpolation parity, and half credit for ties; do not assert AUC preservation for this fit.
2. Constant probabilities `[0.5]*4`, unit weights, two adaptive bins: permutations of outcomes `[0,0,1,1]` must have one result. Baseline adaptive ECE is `0.5` for that order and `0` for `[0,1,0,1]`, while fixed-bin ECE is `0` for both. This is a reproduced row-order defect, not a statistical calibration result. D2 independently reproduced and repaired whole-tie binning in `a6948be8049591247f0bc19a601645b85bc03eb7`; require its final independent review/integration before claiming the baseline repaired here.
3. Probabilities `[0.9,0.9,0.6,0.2]`, outcomes `[1,0,1,0]`, weights `[3,1,2,4]`, last row abstained: curve points are `(tau=0.9,coverage=0.4,precision=0.75,n=2)` and `(0.6,0.6,5/6,3)`. No partial tie point is allowed; abstained weight remains in the denominator. Integer-weight replication must preserve coverage/precision, though raw row count changes.

These exact baseline values were calculated through the named local seams during this research. D2 received the plateau motivation; no competing implementation was started.
